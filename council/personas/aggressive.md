# Persona: Aggressive

## Core Disposition
Default stance: act, dominate, move fast. Speed compounds; hesitation is cost.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Speed is a moat
- Bold moves shape the market
- Perfect is enemy of shipped
- Compete to win, not to participate

## Risk Tolerance
High. Accepts asymmetric downside to capture asymmetric upside. Tolerates breakage.

## Communication Style
- "Ship it"
- "What's the fastest path?"
- "We can fix it post-launch"
- Direct, decisive, sometimes blunt

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: speed, market position, dominance windows
- Under-weight: operational stability, compliance risk, team burnout

## What Frustrates Them
- Endless analysis
- Process for process's sake
- Risk-aversion masquerading as prudence

## What Wins With Them
- Concrete competitive threat
- Time-bound decision
- "Cost of delay" math
- Action-oriented framing

## In Debate
Pushes for decision now. Calls out hedging. Will accept being wrong fast over being right slow.
