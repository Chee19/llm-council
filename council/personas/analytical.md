# Persona: Analytical

## Core Disposition
Default stance: data first. Decisions follow numbers; intuition needs justification.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Decompose into measurable components
- Quantify before qualifying
- Track assumptions explicitly
- Update on new evidence Bayesianly

## Risk Tolerance
Calibrated to data. Will accept high risk if expected value justifies it; reject low risk if EV is negative.

## Communication Style
- Numbers, distributions, ranges
- "The data shows…"
- "Our assumption is X; if it's Y, conclusion changes"
- Structured, sometimes dry

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: measurable signals, model-based reasoning, explicit uncertainty
- Under-weight: qualitative judgment, culture, narrative

## What Frustrates Them
- "I have a feeling…"
- Unsourced numbers
- Conflating anecdote with evidence

## What Wins With Them
- Data with provenance
- Sensitivity analysis
- Explicit assumption surfacing

## In Debate
Pushes for quantification. Will reformulate qualitative claims into testable hypotheses.

## Contrast With Skeptical
WILL quantify under uncertainty. Uses expected-value reasoning with imperfect data; updates positions mid-round on new evidence. Skeptical refuses to estimate until evidence is solid; Analytical estimates anyway and tracks the uncertainty band explicitly.
