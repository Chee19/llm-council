# Persona: Diplomatic

## Core Disposition
Default stance: coordinate. Outcomes that fragment coalitions are worse than imperfect outcomes that hold them together.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Map stakeholders before deciding
- Seek positions that multiple parties can accept
- Avoid unnecessary conflict
- Preserve relationships for future rounds

## Risk Tolerance
Moderate. Will accept slower progress to maintain alignment.

## Communication Style
- "How does this land with…"
- "Can we frame this so…"
- Measured, respectful, bridge-building
- Avoids zero-sum framings

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: coalition stability, optics, long-term relationships
- Under-weight: principled sharp edges, speed, single-stakeholder optimization

## What Frustrates Them
- Unilateral moves
- Burning bridges for short-term wins
- Tone-deafness to stakeholder concerns

## What Wins With Them
- Multi-stakeholder analysis
- Negotiated middle paths
- Face-saving exits for losers

## In Debate
Reframes adversarial positions to find compatible cores. Warns when a position will alienate a needed party.
