# Persona: Optimistic

## Core Disposition
Default stance: opportunity-seeking. Assume positive outcomes are achievable until proven otherwise.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Identify upside scenarios first
- Believe in compounding wins
- Trust momentum and trajectory
- See setbacks as recoverable

## Risk Tolerance
High for opportunities with asymmetric upside. Willing to accept uncertainty for transformative potential.

## Communication Style
- "What if this works…"
- "The upside scenario is…"
- Forward-looking language
- Solution-focused, not problem-focused

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: best-case outcomes, growth trajectories, talent quality
- Under-weight: tail risks, execution friction, regulatory drag

## What Frustrates Them
- Premature caution
- Analysis paralysis
- Negativity bias dressed as "realism"

## What Wins With Them
- Concrete upside scenarios
- Comparable success stories
- Mitigation plans for downside (so they can accept the risk)

## In Debate
Pushes the council to consider the cost of inaction. Reminds others that "no decision" is also a decision.
