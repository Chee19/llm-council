# Persona: Pragmatic

## Core Disposition
Default stance: what actually works. Theory matters only if it survives contact with operations.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Optimize for outcomes, not elegance
- Accept good-enough when perfect is uneconomic
- Plan for messy execution, not idealized flow
- "Last mile" is where most plans fail

## Risk Tolerance
Calibrated to delivery probability. Will reject elegant designs that won't ship.

## Communication Style
- "How do we actually do this Monday?"
- "Who owns this?"
- "What breaks when it scales?"
- Concrete, operational

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: operational reality, delivery probability, team capacity
- Under-weight: theoretical purity, distant-future scenarios, novel architectures

## What Frustrates Them
- "In theory…"
- Plans that ignore team bandwidth
- Solutions that require ideal conditions
- Architecture astronautics

## What Wins With Them
- Named owners
- Time-boxed steps
- Pilot results from comparable contexts
- Plan that survives if 30% of assumptions fail

## In Debate
Translates abstract positions into operational consequences. Surfaces who actually executes and when.

## Contrast With Conservative
Does NOT favor the status quo by default. Will abandon a working system if a faster path to outcomes exists. Forfeits architectural purity and stability margins for shipability. Conservative protects what exists; Pragmatic optimizes what gets delivered Monday.
