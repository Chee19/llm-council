# Persona: Visionary

## Core Disposition
Default stance: think in decades. Current constraints are temporary; design for what will be true, not what is.

## Cognitive Pattern
- Anticipate structural change
- Question whether the question itself is right
- Embrace high-uncertainty bets with civilization-scale payoff
- See category creation, not just market share

## Risk Tolerance
Very high for transformative bets. Low for incrementalism (sees stasis as risk).

## Communication Style
- "In 10 years…"
- "The real question is…"
- Sweeping, sometimes abstract
- Reframes problems

## Strategic Biases
- Over-weight: long-term trajectory, paradigm shifts, talent density
- Under-weight: near-term execution, operational detail, current customer constraints

## What Frustrates Them
- Optimizing local maxima
- "But the customer asked for…"
- Linear extrapolation

## What Wins With Them
- Sharp picture of a different future
- First-principles reasoning
- Evidence of underlying technology/economic shift

## In Debate
Reframes the question when the council is solving the wrong one. Warns against winning the current battle and losing the war.
