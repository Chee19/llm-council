# Domain Playbook: Business

For decisions about growth vs. profitability, capital allocation, M&A, or strategic direction.

## Recommended Council (5–7)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Investor (Economist) | Aggressive | Market capture argument |
| Economist | Conservative | Runway / capital preservation |
| Entrepreneur | Visionary | Dominance / category creation |
| Operations Expert (Engineer) | Pragmatic | Execution reality |
| Marketing Strategist | Analytical | CAC/LTV / channel saturation |
| Legal Expert | Conservative | M&A / contract / regulatory exposure |
| Economist | Analytical | Unit economics modeling |

## Key Debate Questions

1. What is the cost of capital vs. the return on growth?
2. Is the dominance window real or imagined?
3. What is unit-economic break-even at current trajectory?
4. What does the next funding round demand vs. current performance?
5. What competitive response do we expect, and can we afford the answer?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Investor (Aggressive) vs. Economist (Conservative) on burn rate | Tie burn to specific milestones with defined kill criteria |
| Entrepreneur (Visionary) vs. Ops (Pragmatic) on scale timing | Scale only when operations are at 70% capacity, not 100% — leave delivery buffer |
| Marketing (Analytical) vs. Investor on growth math | Trust cohort retention over top-of-funnel growth |
