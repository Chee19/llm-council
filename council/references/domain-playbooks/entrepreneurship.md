# Domain Playbook: Entrepreneurship

For founder decisions about pivots, PMF, fundraising, or strategic bets.

## Recommended Council (4–6)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Entrepreneur | Visionary | Defend current vision |
| Entrepreneur | Skeptical | Challenge PMF assumptions |
| Marketing Strategist (Market Analyst) | Analytical | PMF signal analysis |
| Economist (Finance Expert) | Conservative | Runway / dilution math |
| Product Strategist (Entrepreneur) | Pragmatic | Customer-validated pivot path |
| Engineer | Optimistic | Technical feasibility of pivot direction (substitute AI Researcher if pivot is AI-native) |

## Key Debate Questions

1. What is the PMF signal — retention, organic growth, willingness to pay?
2. What customer problem is top-3 for your buyer?
3. Is the pivot adjacent (same customer, new problem) or adventurous (new customer)?
4. What runway remains, and does the pivot fit inside it?
5. What did the founder's prior conviction cost the company already?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Entrepreneur (Visionary) vs. Entrepreneur (Skeptical) on persistence | Cohort retention beats founder conviction; if D30 retention < threshold, pivot |
| Market Analyst (Analytical) vs. Founder on signal interpretation | Disaggregate "growth" — organic vs. paid; engaged vs. logged-in |
| Finance (Conservative) vs. Visionary on raise-and-extend | Raise must be justified by a milestone, not by burn-rate accommodation |
