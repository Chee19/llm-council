# Domain Playbook: Healthcare

For decisions about clinical AI, health products, care delivery, or medical startups.

## Recommended Council (5–7)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Medical Expert | Conservative | Patient-safety anchor |
| Medical Expert | Skeptical | Probes diagnostic claims |
| Entrepreneur | Visionary | Market opportunity framing |
| Legal Expert | Analytical | Liability and FDA pathway |
| AI Researcher | Optimistic | Capability assessment |
| Regulator | Conservative | Enforcement reality |
| Economist | Pragmatic | Reimbursement viability |

Optional: Ethics Expert (Ethical) for consent / vulnerable populations.

## Key Debate Questions

1. What is the harm if the system is wrong?
2. Is the intended use diagnostic, decision-support, or administrative? (Determines regulatory pathway.)
3. What is the reimbursement model?
4. Who carries liability — clinician, hospital, vendor?
5. What is the post-market surveillance plan?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Entrepreneur (Visionary) vs. Medical (Conservative) on launch timing | Default to physician-assistive scope, not standalone diagnostic |
| AI Researcher (Optimistic) vs. Regulator (Conservative) on validation | Validation must match deployment distribution, not benchmark |
| Economist (Pragmatic) vs. Medical (Skeptical) on cost-effectiveness claims | Demand cohort retention + outcome data, not utilization metrics |
