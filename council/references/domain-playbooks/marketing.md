# Domain Playbook: Marketing

For decisions about positioning, channel strategy, message framing, or advertising tactics.

## Recommended Council (4–6)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing Strategist | Aggressive | High-conversion tactic push |
| Marketing Strategist | Analytical | Funnel + attribution modeling |
| Ethics Expert | Ethical | Manipulation / consent constraint |
| Brand Strategist (Marketing Strategist) | Diplomatic | Long-term reputation |
| Psychologist (Scientist) | Analytical | Behavioral mechanism evidence |
| Legal Expert | Conservative | Disclosure / advertising standards |

Note: Marketing role is **morally neutral** (see role file). The Ethical persona / Ethics Expert provides the constraint.

## Key Debate Questions

1. What customer belief are we shifting?
2. Does the tactic erode brand trust at scale?
3. Is the manipulation transparent or covert?
4. What is the regulatory disclosure requirement?
5. Does this work because users are uninformed, or despite being informed?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Marketing (Aggressive) vs. Ethics (Ethical) on manipulation | Distinguish persuasion from deception; deception is rejected, even-if-legal |
| Brand (Diplomatic) vs. Marketing (Aggressive) on short-term lift | Calculate brand-equity cost; do not externalize it from CAC math |
| Marketing (Analytical) on attribution vs. Psychologist on causation | Trust experimentally-validated lift over correlational attribution |

## Source Example
Concept doc §13 — "Should emotionally manipulative advertising be used?" → Rejected for trust erosion and behavioral manipulation risk.
