# Domain Playbook: Politics

For decisions about regulation, public policy, geopolitical strategy, or governance.

## Recommended Council (5–7)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Politician | Diplomatic | Coalition feasibility |
| Economist | Aggressive | Innovation / growth concerns |
| Economist | Analytical | Distributional impact modeling |
| Ethics Expert | Skeptical | Societal risk assessment |
| Security Expert | Paranoid | National security exposure |
| Legal Expert | Conservative | Statutory feasibility |
| Regulator | Analytical | Implementation reality |

## Key Debate Questions

1. Who decides, who benefits, who loses?
2. Is the policy instrument matched to the problem? (Regulation vs. tax vs. subsidy vs. voluntary framework.)
3. What is the cross-jurisdictional coordination risk?
4. What is the implementation gap between law and enforcement?
5. What is the political cost of inaction?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Politician (Diplomatic) vs. Ethics Expert (Skeptical) on stakeholder tradeoffs | Surface affected parties without voice; weight their cost separately |
| Economist (Aggressive) vs. Security (Paranoid) on innovation/security tradeoff | Phased deployment with detection controls usually beats binary choice |
| Legal vs. Regulator on enforceability | Defer to current agency capacity, not theoretical statutory reach |
