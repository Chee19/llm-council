# Domain Playbook: Science

For decisions about research direction, dual-use research, methodology, or scientific policy.

## Recommended Council (4–6)

| Role | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Scientist | Analytical | Methodology rigor |
| Scientist | Skeptical | Replication / falsifiability pressure |
| Ethics Expert | Conservative | Restraint on enhancement / dual-use |
| Entrepreneur | Visionary | Translational / commercial opportunity |
| Medical Expert | Conservative | Clinical safety (if biomedical) |
| Regulator | Analytical | Compliance pathway (if applicable) |

## Key Debate Questions

1. What is the falsifiable claim?
2. What is the replication status?
3. What dual-use risk does this create?
4. Is the methodology appropriate to the question?
5. What is the ethical scope (enhancement vs. therapy, human vs. animal, consenting vs. vulnerable)?

## Common Deadlocks

| Deadlock | Resolution Heuristic |
|---|---|
| Scientist (Analytical) vs. Ethics (Conservative) on continuing research | Distinguish basic research from translation; constrain translation, not inquiry |
| Entrepreneur (Visionary) vs. Scientist (Skeptical) on translational timing | Wait for replicated effect size, not single-paper hype |
| Dual-use disagreement | Default to publication restraint when adversarial use is concrete and proximate |

## Source Example
Concept doc §13 — "Should human gene editing continue?" → Continue research, restrict enhancement editing, safety-first translation.
